Squad value: the sum of the model prices (no buyer) of the club's players listed on the site, on 1 July 2026. "When it sells" compares the fees the club obtained since 2014 with those other clubs obtained, the same years, for players with the same Transfermarkt value. Grey means too few sales or a difference that could be chance.
