Player fee model
PlayersClubs

What would a club pay
for this player?

The transfer fee each player would be expected to fetch on 1 July 2026, estimated by a model trained on past paid transfers. Pick a buying club to see the price for that club, and click a player to see how his price is built.

        How to read these numbers
        • The model price is an estimate of a transfer fee, not a quality rating. On real transfers it had never seen (2024–2026), the version without a buyer was off by a median of about 41%.
        • With a buying club chosen, the price comes from the full model, which also knows the buyer: its country and league, its spending, its squad value and what it usually pays compared with Transfermarkt values. It is more accurate on real transfers (median error about 38%).
        • A price shown as a rough guess is an unusual deal: the player's Transfermarkt value is below the cheapest 5% or above the most expensive of the players that clubs of that league bought over the last five years, so the model has little to go on.
        • The range under each price is an 80% interval: for four in five real transfers, the actual fee fell inside it.
        • Each yearly price uses only transfers that happened before that date. The club shown for a year is the club the player was registered with on 1 July of that year, a move dated that day included. A player on loan on that date is shown at the club he was loaned to; a loan that ended on 30 June counts as ended.
        • The model learns from players who were actually sold, and it knows nothing about contract length, so prices for players who are not for sale are less reliable.
        • The transfers of a player show the fee when one was paid and published. The source does not say whether a move is a loan, so this is worked out from the moves themselves: a move followed by a free return to the same club is a loan, the return is the end of the loan, and a move with no fee and no return is a free transfer.
        • Data, club crests and player photos: Transfermarkt, via the open transfermarkt-datasets project. The data stops on 6 July 2026. A short list of major later moves (the biggest transfers of the summer and the main free agents) was added by hand so that those players appear at their new club; other moves made after 6 July 2026 are missing.
